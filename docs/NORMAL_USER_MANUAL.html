VERSION 4 VERIFIED WORKFLOW

Normal User Manual — Dining Counter Patron Guide

This manual is intended for student diners and dining counter operators using the public Verify Meal tab of the EEE 312 Voice Verification Kiosk.

1. Quick Start: How to Verify at the Counter

Step 1 — Initiate Verification. Press Record and Verify once. Do not double-click.
Step 2 — Watch Visual Countdown (3... 2... 1...). Prepare to speak.
Step 3 — Remain Silent for 0.5-Second Lead-In. When recording begins, remain completely silent for 0.5 seconds while the system profiles ambient cafeteria noise. Speaking prematurely during this silence will corrupt the noise subtraction filter!
Step 4 — Speak Your 7-Digit Student ID. When the display shows >>> SPEAK NOW! <<<, clearly speak your seven-digit Student ID at normal conversational pace (e.g. "Two, Two, Zero, Six, One, Four, Seven").
Step 5 — Automatic Name Retry (If Needed). If your spoken ID is ambiguous or borderline, the system will prompt you for your Spoken Full Name. Follow the same silent lead-in and speak your name.
Step 6 — Monthly Coupon Token (First Meal of the Month Only).
If dining for the first time in a new calendar month, enter the 6-digit coupon token assigned by the hall administration in the 6-digit coupon code field. Once accepted, dining is unlocked for the next 30 days — you will not be asked for a coupon again this month.
Step 7 — Collect Meal. When the screen displays a green SERVE [Meal] confirmation, collect your plate.

2. Dynamic Monthly Coupon Policy (Version 4 Update)

Dining Situation Required Patron Action
First meal in a new calendar month Speak your Student ID, and type your dynamic 6-digit monthly coupon token into the coupon box. The system consumes this token and records your 30-day dining entitlement.
Subsequent meals in the same month Speak your Student ID only. Leave the coupon box empty. The system automatically recognizes your active 30-day payment record.
Second meal in the same service window Identity may verify, but the meal is REFUSED by the single-serving hall rule (only one meal per student per window).
Expired or reused coupon REFUSED. Every monthly coupon is strictly bound to one student, one month, and one successful transaction.

3. Manual Fallback Procedure

If severe hoarseness, cold, or microphone faults prevent voice matching after two attempts:

  1. Type your 7-digit Student ID in the box labeled: If voice matching fails: type ID/name.
  2. If this is your first meal of the month, ensure your 6-digit coupon token is typed into the coupon box.
  3. Press Manual entry. The system validates your enrollment, active meal hours, and monthly dining entitlement.

5. Hygiene & Privacy Notice

This system provides a 100% touchless acoustic verification interface to protect communal health in university dining halls. Voice biometrics are processed purely locally on edge hardware and are never uploaded to external internet servers.
